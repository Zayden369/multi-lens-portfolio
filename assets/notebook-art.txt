Notebook Draft display alphabet and Sketchbook technical illustrations
Original artwork authored for this portfolio, 2026.

The alphabet centerlines and generator live in tools/build_drafting_font.py.
It uses the existing project's dependency-free TrueType packing helpers.
It contains no outlines from the downloaded reference fonts.
The generated font and original SVG/HTML illustrations may be redistributed
with this project's source. No third-party images or font files are bundled.

Use the display alphabet for short titles; lowercase deliberately maps to
capitals. Body copy, controls, and technical labels use system fonts.
